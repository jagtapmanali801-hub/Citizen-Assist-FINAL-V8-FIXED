    Why we created it

    Making digital government services easier to understand

    Many government services are available online, but users can feel confused about where to start, which documents to keep ready, what each step means and which website is the correct official destination. Citizen Assist is designed as a simple guidance layer between the citizen and the official portal.

    The website does not replace government portals. It explains the process in simple language, helps users prepare and then directs them to the official website for the actual application, payment, login or submission.

    Our ideaExplain first. Prepare clearly. Continue safely.

    Who it is for

    Built for everyday digital tasks

    Whether someone is applying for an identity document, checking a scholarship, preparing a certificate or opening a digital service, the site keeps the journey focused on one task at a time.

    How it stays useful

    Clear guidance with official destinations

    Service information is organised into preparation, steps, safety notes and official links. Because government rules can change, users are reminded to confirm current details on the destination portal.

    Accessibility first

    More ways to read and navigate

    The website supports language switching, larger text, contrast controls, dark mode, simple view and spoken page reading to make the information easier to use.

    Important to remember

    Citizen Assist is a student educational/community-engagement project. Government rules, fees, documents and processes can change. Always verify the latest information on the official government website before submitting an application.
