This is a student Community Engagement Project website for:
“Assisting Citizens in Using Government Digital Portals.”

Latest fixes included:
- Citizen Assist name and cropped logo are clearly visible in the sticky header.
- AI Assistance logo/button is fixed on every page while scrolling.
- AI chat supports English, Hindi and Marathi interface responses.
- Basic HTML View, High Contrast Theme, Invert Colors, Font Size and Saturation controls work and persist.
- English/Hindi/Marathi switching updates the main interface, navigation, footer, service directory, guides, FAQ, popup and AI assistant.
- Service directory expanded to 14 services across identity, transport/travel, education, welfare and digital citizen services.
- Service search, role filtering, category filtering and difficulty filtering work.
- Every service has a guide, document preparation list, step checklist, official destination and tracking link.
- Official government website text link is blue and underlined.
- Home page includes an attractive scrolling/auto-rotating project visual story explaining Citizen Assist.
- About page includes project purpose, problem, workflow, objectives, AI assistance and a visible Explore Services CTA.
- Welcome popup and guided tour are included.
- No government logo or government identity is presented as the site's own identity.

The actual application/payment/login should always be completed on the relevant official government portal.
Citizen Assist should never ask users for OTPs, passwords, PINs or bank credentials.
